# Style reference cards

Curated **“in the spirit of”** briefs. Use interval *shapes* and feel — **not** exact melodies.

Format: `keywords` → compose with Strudel + GM sounds unless noted.

---

## iron man | black sabbath | doom metal riff | heavy metal intro

- **Tempo:** 70–80 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** B minor (or E minor)
- **Feel:** slow, heavy, deliberate; palm-muted quarters; space between hits
- **Motif shape:** short repeating minor cell; root ↔ b5 tension; power fifths; 2–3 note cells only
- **Layers:** distorted guitar riff (`gm_distortion_guitar`), bass doubling root/fifth (`gm_electric_bass_finger`), rock kit (`power-kit` or `standard-kit`)
- **Avoid:** fast 16th fills, major pop brightness, EDM four-on-floor, busy melody

---

## daft punk | french house | discovery era

- **Tempo:** 120–125 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** F minor or A minor
- **Feel:** four-on-floor kick; syncopated off-beat bass; tight disco grid
- **Bass:** monophonic syncopated line (`bassline` synth OR `gm_synth_bass_1`); octave jumps on bar 2
- **Drums:** `electronic-kit`; bd four-on-floor; sd on 2 and 4; crisp hh off-beats
- **Avoid:** rock backbeat only, acoustic kits, slow ballad harmony

---

## hans zimmer | cinematic epic | blockbuster brass

- **Tempo:** 80–110 BPM (half-time feel ok), 4/4
- **Key:** D minor or E minor
- **Feel:** wide dynamics; long notes; ostinato strings under brass swells
- **Layers:** low string ostinato (`gm_string_ensemble_1`), brass stabs (`gm_brass_section`), taiko/low percussion optional (`gm_taiko_drum` / low timpani color)
- **Harmony:** i – bVI – bIII – bVII modal minor; power chords sparingly
- **Avoid:** funk syncopation, 808 trap, chiptune

---

## john williams | adventure fanfare | hero theme spirit

- **Tempo:** 100–120 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** Bb major or C major
- **Feel:** triumphant; dotted rhythms; ascending fourths/fifths; clear strong beats
- **Motif shape:** bugle-like ascending interval (4th or 5th), rest, repeat; not a known film melody
- **Layers:** trumpet fanfare (`gm_trumpet`), french horn support (`gm_french_horn`), timpani rolls (`gm_timpani`)
- **Avoid:** metal distortion, lo-fi dust, minimal techno

---

## lo-fi hip hop | chill beats | study beats

- **Tempo:** 70–90 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** D minor or F major (jazzy borrowed chords ok)
- **Feel:** swung or laid-back drums; sparse kick; soft snare; vinyl mood via mix not notes
- **Layers:** electric piano chords (`gm_electric_piano_1`), simple bass root movement (`gm_acoustic_bass`), brushed kit (`brush-kit` or `jazz-kit`)
- **Harmony:** ii–V–i color or I–vi–ii–V with 7ths
- **Avoid:** heavy metal riffing, four-on-floor house, aggressive brass

---

## trap | 808 beat | modern hip hop

- **Tempo:** 130–150 BPM (half-time feel), 4/4
- **Key:** F minor or C minor
- **Feel:** sparse kick; rolling hi-hats (16ths); hard snare/clap on 3 (half-time)
- **Drums:** `electronic-kit`; sub-heavy kick pattern; minimal melodic density
- **Bass:** low 808-style roots (`gm_synth_bass_2`); long sustained notes
- **Avoid:** four-on-floor house kick, jazz swing, orchestral layers

---

## funk | james brown | tight groove

- **Tempo:** 95–110 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** E minor or A minor (Dorian color)
- **Feel:** syncopated 16ths on guitar/bass; emphasis on the one; ghost notes
- **Layers:** muted guitar chops (`gm_electric_guitar_muted`), slap or finger bass (`gm_slap_bass_1`), tight kit with ghost snare (`jazz-kit` or `standard-kit`)
- **Harmony:** one-chord vamps or i7; avoid long progressions
- **Avoid:** straight rock eighths only, ballad tempo, orchestral swells

---

## vaporwave | 80s ballad spirit | retro synth

- **Tempo:** 80–100 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** Eb major or Ab major
- **Feel:** slow; lush; simple I–vi–IV–V; long sustains
- **Layers:** DX-style synth pads (`heisenberg` or `gm_synth_strings_1`), soft electric piano, gentle drum machine (`analog-kit`)
- **Avoid:** metal distortion, fast arpeggios, trap hats

---

## aphex twin | idm | glitch spirit

- **Tempo:** 120–140 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** atonal or A minor; irregular phrase lengths
- **Feel:** broken beats; syncopated kicks; not four-on-floor
- **Layers:** acid bass (`bassline`), electronic kit with irregular kick placement, short digital lead bleeps (`pulsar` / `gm_lead_2_square`)
- **Avoid:** predictable I–V–vi–IV pop, orchestral brass, slow doom metal

---

## billie eilish | whisper pop | sparse dark pop

- **Tempo:** 80–95 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** G minor or C minor
- **Feel:** minimal; sub-heavy; few notes; lots of space
- **Layers:** sub bass (`gm_synth_bass_1`), soft kick pattern, sparse hi-hats, simple minor motif (3–5 notes max)
- **Avoid:** busy funk guitar, triumphant brass, fast metal

---

## reggaeton | dembow

- **Tempo:** 90–98 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** A minor or D minor
- **Feel:** dembow rhythm (kick-snare syncopation); off-beat bass
- **Drums:** `electronic-kit`; characteristic dembow snare pattern (not generic rock backbeat)
- **Bass:** simple minor roots with rhythmic repeats
- **Avoid:** four-on-floor house, swing jazz, metal palm mute

---

## bossa nova | brazilian jazz spirit

- **Tempo:** 120–140 BPM (bossa subdivision), 4/4
- **Key:** F major or Bb major
- **Feel:** nylon guitar syncopation; gentle brush kit
- **Layers:** nylon guitar (`gm_acoustic_guitar_nylon`), brush kit (`brush-kit`), walking-ish bass (`gm_acoustic_bass`)
- **Harmony:** maj7 / ii7 colors; II–V–I jazz turns
- **Avoid:** distortion, trap 808, heavy metal

---

## video game chiptune | 8-bit spirit

- **Tempo:** 120–160 BPM, 4/4
- **Key:** C major or A minor
- **Feel:** square/triangle leads; arpeggiated bass; simple triadic harmony
- **Layers:** `tonematrix` or `gm_lead_1_square` + `gm_bass_1` + minimal kit
- **Motif shape:** short catchy 4-note cells; upward arpeggios
- **Avoid:** realistic orchestral GM, long reverb tails, complex jazz chords
